Liability Insurance Certificate — Always Required

Current certificate of insurance showing minimum CAD $2,000,000 coverage. Must be valid for at least 30 days from today.

ISO 13485 Certificate — Required for Regulated Categories

Required if supplying: Wound Care, Class I/II/III-IV Medical Devices, or Surgical Instruments. Not required for PPE, Exam Consumables, Facility Supplies, NHP, or Services.

MDEL Certificate — Required for Class II, III-IV and Surgical

Medical Device Establishment Licence required for Class II, Class III-IV Medical Devices, and Surgical Instruments. Class I MD is exempt.

NHP Product Licence — Required for Natural Health Products

Health Canada Natural Health Product licence required if supplying NHP category products.

ISO 9001 Certificate — Optional

If you hold an ISO 9001 certificate, you may upload it. Not required to complete this application.

Trade References — Minimum 1 Required (ISO 7.4)

Company name, contact name, and relationship description for at least one organisation you currently or have previously supplied. This is an ISO 7.4 supplier evaluation requirement.
