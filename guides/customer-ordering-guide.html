Ordering with Essential Cures: a guide for customers

From quotation to delivery, every step of your order happens through secure links we email to you. This guide explains what to expect and how to keep track of your order.
Current version

How an order moves

  1. 1

    Review your proposal or quotation

    We send you a quotation, and for larger orders a proposal first. Where two options are offered, review both and select the one that suits you best, online in a few clicks.

  2. 2

    Provide your purchase order and advance payment

    If your organization issues purchase orders, submit yours online or by email. Where an advance payment applies, record it on the same page.

  3. 3

    Receive your order confirmation

    Once your purchase order and any advance payment are verified, we confirm your order by email with an estimated delivery window in business days.

  4. 4

    Track your order

    Your order page shows each shipment as it progresses. Choose which updates you would like to receive by email. We always notify you of any delay.

  5. 5

    Receive your goods

    Every parcel arrives with an Essential Cures delivery note. Check the goods against the note and keep it, as it records the lot numbers for traceability.

  6. 6

    Receive your invoice

    We invoice each shipment once it is delivered. Your advance payment and any discount are applied proportionally across your invoices.

  7. 7

    Report a problem

    If anything arrives damaged, short or incorrect, report it from your order page or contact our Customer Care team. We arrange a replacement, exchange or credit.

Good to know

  • Orders may arrive in more than one parcel, each with its own delivery note.
  • Delivery estimates are in business days, counted from your order confirmation.
  • Delivery notes never show prices, so they can travel safely with your goods.
  • Your order page stays available until your order is complete, then remains viewable for a limited period.
