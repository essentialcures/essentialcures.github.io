Ordering with Essential Cures: a guide for suppliers

Essential Cures delivers healthcare supplies directly from our approved suppliers to our customers. This guide explains each step of an order and what we ask of you, so every delivery arrives complete, traceable and on time.
Current version

How an order moves

  1. 1

    Confirm the pre-order request

    We send a pre-order confirmation request before we issue a purchase order. For each line, confirm availability and enter your order fulfilment days and transit days. Please respond within the time stated in the request.

  2. 2

    Receive and confirm the purchase order

    Once our customer confirms their order, we email you a purchase order with a secure link. Open the link, review the order and confirm it.

  3. 3

    Advance payment, where your terms require it

    If your terms include an advance payment, we arrange it promptly after you confirm the purchase order and notify you when it is sent.

  4. 4

    Record the shipment on dispatch day

    Open the purchase order link and record each parcel: the carrier, tracking number and quantities shipped, plus the lot or batch number and expiry date for every line.

  5. 5

    Print the Essential Cures delivery note

    The page then generates a delivery note for that parcel. Print it and pack it with the goods. Each parcel needs its own delivery note.

  6. 6

    Ship directly to our customer

    Ship to the delivery address on the purchase order. Partial shipments and backorders are welcome. Record each parcel separately as it leaves.

  7. 7

    Invoice Essential Cures

    Email your invoice to finance@essentialcures.com, quoting our purchase order number. We pay according to your agreed terms.

  8. 8

    Resolve any issues with us

    If a parcel is damaged, short or returned, we will contact you to arrange a replacement, exchange or credit. Replacement parcels are recorded on the same purchase order link.

Packing and conduct rules

  • Include only the Essential Cures delivery note in each parcel.
  • Never include your own invoice, packing slip or price list in the parcel.
  • Quote our purchase order number on all shipping documents and invoices.
  • Record the lot or batch number and expiry date for every line. This keeps every product traceable in the event of a recall.
  • Do not contact our customers directly. All communication about an order goes through Essential Cures.
